# Returns & Refund Policy (demo placeholder)
DEMO PLACEHOLDER. Replace with the real Returns Policy PDF in Supabase Storage.

- Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused, unwashed and with tags attached.
- Raise a return in the Dhaga app: Orders > select the order > Return. Choose the reason that fits best.
- A reverse pickup is scheduled. Refunds are issued after the item passes inspection at the fulfilment centre.
- Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method. COD orders are refunded to a bank account or UPI ID the customer provides.
